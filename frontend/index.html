The problem

Routine questions take time away from people.

Students and parents often need quick answers about admissions, fees, schedules, transport and institutional policies. Staff may have to respond to the same questions repeatedly across calls and messages.

↻

Repeated enquiries

Similar questions can arrive again and again, consuming staff time.

◷

Delayed responses

People may not get an answer immediately, especially outside office hours.

▤

Scattered information

Useful details may be spread across notices, documents and webpages.

The solution

Answers grounded in each institution's information.

InstitutionAI is designed to retrieve relevant information from an institution's knowledge base and use it to help generate a useful response—rather than relying only on generic chatbot knowledge.

⌕

Institution-specific retrieval

Find relevant passages from the information provided for that institution.

✦

AI-generated responses

Use a language model to turn retrieved information into a natural-language answer.

◫

Built for common enquiries

Designed around topics such as admissions, fees, schedules, transport and policies.

How it works

From an institutional document to a helpful answer.

The current prototype combines retrieval and generation in a backend workflow.
